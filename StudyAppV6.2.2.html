App User Manual & Reference

1. Study Modes

  • Learn Mode (Batched): Teaches 3 concepts at a time, then immediately quizzes you on them before moving to the next batch.
  • Standard Mode: Multiple choice with randomized option shuffling.
  • Flashcard Mode: Tap to reveal answer. Swipe left (wrong) or right (correct).
  • Timed Exam Mode: Standard mode enforced with a 15-second countdown timer.
  • Audio Dictation: Click the "🔊 Read" button during any mode to have your device read the question and answer out loud.
  • In-Quiz Deletion: Click the "🗑️ Delete" button in the top-right corner of any study mode to immediately delete an unneeded question and advance to the next one.

2. Organizing Questions & Renaming Banks

  • Rename or Delete Banks: Click the ✏️ or 🗑️ icon next to any bank name inside a subject.
  • Split Bulk Banks into Lecture Banks: Click 🔍 Browse on your bulk bank, check off the questions you want (hold Shift and click to select a range), type a new bank name in the green Organize box, and click ✂️ Move to New Bank or 📋 Copy to New Bank.
  • Transfer Between Existing Banks: Select questions in Browse view, choose a destination bank in the dropdown, and click ✂️ Move to Existing or 📋 Copy to Existing.

3. Multi-Lecture & Multi-Subject Study

You can practice across multiple materials at once:

  • Multi-Bank (Within a Subject): In any subject view, check off the specific lectures/banks you want to combine, choose a mode, and click Start Selected Banks.
  • Multi-Subject Mega Session: On the main Subjects dashboard, select the checkboxes for any subjects you want to combine (e.g. FOREC 345 + RenR 201), pick your mode, and start.

4. Image Hotspot Quizzes (Touch-Targeting)

Transform diagrams into interactive touch-quizzes. In your JSON payload, add a hotspot object containing the x percentage, y percentage, and a radius. The app will automatically hide text options and ask you to tap the image instead.

6. Excel / CSV Importer Guidelines

Set up an Excel spreadsheet with 5 columns in this exact order (do not include header text rows):

  1. Question Text
  2. Correct Answer
  3. Wrong Option 1
  4. Wrong Option 2
  5. Wrong Option 3

Save as a .csv file and upload it in the Manage Data tab.
